## 3. The Quickstep RAM problem: what is known
- [V, CG1F angle report] the Go `IconRecentsView` adds a listener to the process-wide `RecentsModel` in its constructor and never removes it: every NEW Activity of the stock launcher stays pinned
  for the life of the process. The emulator's plain AOSP `RecentsView` does not do that, so no emulator run can show the growth (cd2 / cd3 / cd3n: flat).
- [I, new in this pass: the ORDER of the two saver flips] a configuration change re-creates only an Activity that is VISIBLE; a stopped / invisible one is compared with its last reported
  configuration when it next becomes visible. At the lock the launcher flips battery saver OFF while the keyguard is up (the stock activity is invisible: no re-creation). The restore flip comes
  after a gesture unlock into Quickstep, when the stock activity is already visible again: the config (day) differs from the last reported one (night) at that resume (one re-creation) and the
  restore flip (night again) is a second one. So one lock + gesture unlock with the saver on in Quickstep plausibly pins TWO stock Activities. Tj's tablet: many such cycles a day = hundreds of MB over a day
  or two (his 1-day average 445 MB, the friend's tablet 48 MB). NOT measured on the tablet (needs the QsMemory ring + the BATSAVER count in a report).
- Tj's own force stops (CD1j: 450 -> 280 -> 130 MB, 3-hour average) were the same cure by hand.
- What the app cannot do: kill the process (adj 100; `killBackgroundProcesses` only touches adj >= 500), patch the stock launcher, stop the flips (the lock screen needs the saver off, Tj's rule AC3 / BA2).
- What SR1 does: the same cure, done for Tj, at the one moment nobody uses the stock process (the custom home is on screen). Without the shell helper it does nothing. The first Recents opening or Quickstep entry after
  a restart starts the process cold (about one second, once). A healthy stock launcher (50-130 MB) is never touched.
