A disabled SeekBar never gets `onStopTrackingTouch`, so if Do Not Disturb flips on while a finger is on a slider and a refresh runs, `tracking` stays true and `refreshSoon()` returns early for that row until the page is stopped and started again. | FIX: `if (zen) tracking = false;` right after `setEnabled`. |
| 2 | LOW (confirmed) | `SsPagesStorage.post(gen, full)` always posts `rowSizes(s.apps, s.ext, used)`. When the rescan times out / throws (`s.apps == null`), Games, Movies, Other apps, Photos, Music and System turn UNKNOWN, contradicting the `load()` doc ("previous keeps its rows on screen until the new numbers replace them"). On a tablet whose scan always exceeds 45 s the per-app rows stay blank on every visit. | FIX: when the scan gave no apps/ext and a snapshot is already shown, post `new Snapshot(vol[0], vol[1], shown)`. |
| 3 | LOW (plausible) | `SsPagesBattery.start()` -> `maybeReload(true)` bypasses the 30 s throttle: every Settings home or Battery page start spawns `dumpsys batterystats --charged` (killing the process after "Screen on:" does not stop the work in system_server). Same root as review B item 4 (`SsHomeSummary`). Related: the throttle and the watchdog use `System.currentTimeMillis()`; a clock set backwards stops reloads until the old time passes. | FIX: `maybeReload(false)` on start when data is already there and younger than the throttle; `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime()` for the throttle and the watchdog. |
| 4 | LOW (plausible) | `SsRingtoneFragment.onActivityResult` casts the picker's `EXTRA_RINGTONE_PICKED_URI` with no guard; a third-party app that answers `ACTION_RINGTONE_PICKER` with a non-Uri extra throws on the main thread of the shared launcher process. Not reachable with only the system picker installed. | FIX: `try/catch (RuntimeException)` around the body. |
