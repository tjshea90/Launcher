What the change is meant to do (facts, not a design to defend):
1. PENCIL COVER (PencilRules.SlideGuard and its use in updatePencil): the cover is an opaque white 1-view overlay window put over the quick-settings "Edit" pencil of the system notification panel, to hide the pencil. A user reported a white box on the lock screen after the panel slides back UP. The suspected cause: while the panel slides up, the pencil node is still found at a slightly higher place at each look, and the cover FOLLOWS it with the lag of the look (an accessibility event at most every 40 ms, or a whole-tree search at most every 200 ms, then a relayout and a frame), so the white cover trails the retreating panel over the lock screen. The new rule: when a look finds the pencil more than 3 px higher than at the previous look, the cover is hidden at once and kept away (a re-look is scheduled 60 ms later because accessibility events stop when the panel stops); it comes back when two looks in a row find the pencil at the same top edge, or after 3 s as a safety valve; a gap of more than 800 ms between looks starts a fresh guard. Moving DOWN (panel opening) is followed as before.
2. LOCK LOG DIGEST (LockLog.digest, LockLog.tailDigest, ReportState): the tablet report's lock-log excerpt folds every run of consecutive "DIRTY" lines into one line and prints the last 100 folded lines (was the last 40 raw lines). Lines look like `HH:mm:ss.SSS EVENT detail`. Pure string code.
3. DIRTY LOG THROTTLE (RecentsDirty.shouldLog, used in TjAccessibilityService.trackDirty): at most one DIRTY lock-log line per package per minute, with a bounded map.
4. RECENTS FAILURE DIAGNOSTICS (TjAccessibilityService.recentsDiag, nodeSummary, walkSummary, recentsActionOk): when the on-screen "clear Recents" routine finds nothing (it gives up after 2.5 s), the report line now also lists the windows (type, layer, root package) and, for windows of package com.android.launcher3, node class counts and up to 8 short texts. ## RESULT (the review DID finish after all: 2026-11-10 ~01:25 UTC, 40 tool uses, 281k subagent tokens; do NOT rerun it)

1. MEDIUM, `TjAccessibilityService.recentsDiag` / `nodeSummary` / `walkSummary` (`texts{}`): the Recents diagnostic breaks the repo's privacy rule. It echoes the first 8 `getText()` / `getContentDescription()` values (24 characters each) of every `com.android.launcher3` node; in Recents those are card descriptions (app label plus page or conversation title). `describeCards` (v0.7.6) refuses to log exactly that, and `tools/tablet_intake.py` redacts only the text after "saw N card(s):", so the texts would reach the PUBLIC repo through `reset_last`. Fix: emit only fixed tokens ("clear all", "no recent items") plus counts.
2. MEDIUM, `PencilRules.SlideGuard.allowCover` and `TjAccessibilityService.updatePencil`: "two looks agree" has no minimum spacing. A hold look arms the 60 ms relook while event-driven looks run every 40 ms or more (event look at t=50, relook at t=60): looks 10 ms apart can read the same SystemUI frame and the same top, so `still` is true, the cover is shown mid-slide and hidden by the next look about 40 ms later (a short white flash). `PencilRulesTest` only uses 60 ms gaps. Fix: `still` counts only when `nowMs - lastMs >= 40`; a look closer than that must not move the anchor (`lastTop` / `lastMs`) either, except that a move UP is still a move up.
3. LOW, `recentsDiag` vs `ReportState.format`: `format()` cuts every value at 600 characters and about 119 characters of `reset_last` come before the diagnostic; the RESET lock-log line (`LockLog.line` caps at 200, about 127 characters of prefix) keeps only about 70 characters. Our own overlay and SystemUI windows are probably listed before the launcher3 entry, so mostly the window list reaches the report. Fix: launcher3 summary first, each window entry about 25 characters.
4. LOW (not a regression, no fix): the guard reacts after the fact: an upward move is noticed at the next look, up to 150 ms later (`penWatch`) if SystemUI sends no event; after the 3 s valve one look passes while the pencil still moves.

No finding: SlideGuard state machine (opening, rest, jitter, reversal, stale reset, valve, sentinel; the 7 tests trace correctly); `penPending` / `penEval` / `penWatch` scheduling (no hot loop, the chain ends when the pencil is lost or released); threading; node recycling in `recentsDiag` / `walkSummary`; `LockLog.digest` (short DIRTY-at-13 lines, empty, null, n <= 0, 200 cap); `RecentsDirty.shouldLog` and the `trackDirty` throttle; callers and signatures (none changed).
Not reviewed: emulator section `b6` / BK1b timing under the new guard; the size effect of 100 lock-log lines on the full report beyond the `TabletReport` caps (watch gate check PG3: the short summary must stay under 12 KB).
