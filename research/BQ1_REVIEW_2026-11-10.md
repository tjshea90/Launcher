# BQ1 / BQ2 review (2026-11-10): two read-only reviewers on the v0.9.2 diff against v0.9.1 (2f5831f); verdicts and decisions

Briefed neutrally (facts, diff command, what to hunt, output format). Both found real problems; all were fixed or decided below. The next session needs no re-read of the transcripts.

## Scrim measurement (BQ1a / BQ2b), 6 findings + design remarks
1. MEDIUM - `scrimTeardown` (onUnbind / onDestroy / onInterrupt) and process death skip `scrimFinish`, so a started run (one real screenshot already taken) was never counted and the new instance (scrimThisLock 0, scrimLastStartMs 0) could start again in the same lock. FIXED: the run is COUNTED WHEN IT STARTS (`addFailure` next to the TRIES_KEY write in `maybeMeasureScrim`); nothing at the end can be lost. `measurementFailure` and `scrimCounted` are gone.
2. LOW - two synchronous `commit()` writes on the main thread at every interruption (the unlock path CLAUDE.md keeps free of sync writes). FIXED: the end-of-run record uses `setStringAsync`.
3. LOW - success detected by the display prefix "MEASURED" (reword = every success counts as a failure). MOOT: nothing is counted at the end any more.
4. LOW - the report printed a failure count that may belong to an old wallpaper signature. FIXED: the report prints the stored record (with its signature) and the current signature.
5. LOW - a benign race could label a stored run "stopped". FIXED by dropping the once-per-run flag: the LAST outcome of a run wins in `scrim_last_run`.
6. LOW (design) - settle anchored at wake / connect, not at the last theme flip. NOT CHANGED: `onConfigurationChanged` already waits 6 s after any flip before it looks again, and BQ2 means a run only ever starts in the light look.
Design remark (cooldown for "stopped" runs instead of a permanent stop after 2): NOT ADOPTED. Tj's rule is "measure once, never screenshot again"; a lock screen whose two tries are both interrupted stays on the generic dimming until 'Measure again' (Launcher control says so). Test suggestion adopted as scenario checks in section pt phase C (run counted at start; `scrim_last_run` says MEASURED, light theme).

## Pencil cover (BQ1b), 7 findings
1. MEDIUM - when the followed node is LOST the whole-tree search (0.5-1.3 s) ran BEFORE the hide, so a non-touch collapse (or a drag whose DOWN came while the cover was hidden/untouchable) left the cover up the whole search. FIXED: `findPencil` hides the cover before it searches.
2. MEDIUM - the finger wait ended only at a look that found the pencil; a closing swipe never gets one, so a pull-down within 3 s was uncovered for the whole pull (events keep the wait alive). FIXED: a full search that finds no pencil ends the wait (`penFinger.reset()`).
3. MEDIUM - ACTION_OUTSIDE fires once at DOWN and not while the window is NOT_TOUCHABLE: a finger that rests about 550 ms and then slides is not announced; the watch restarted slow. FIXED: the first 600 ms after a re-show that followed a finger are watched fast (`penAfterFinger`).
4. LOW - the hold loop was re-armed only while a pencil was found; one failed refresh at +350 ms could leave the cover hidden until the next SystemUI event. FIXED: while the finger wait is on (3 s at most) the look is re-booked even when nothing was found.
5. LOW - both guards were fed the END time of a look; a slow look (450 ms) showed the cover at a position read up to 450 ms earlier. FIXED: the guards get the START time of the look, and a look slower than 150 ms (`PencilRules.staleLook`) does not show or move the cover: it books a quick re-look instead (the node is known then, the re-look is one fast refresh).
6. LOW - `penEval` never re-checked `coverWorld()` / the switch / demo mode: a 350 ms eval could re-create a removed cover. FIXED at the top of `penEval`.
7. LOW - one 250 ms gate shared by 'shown' and 'hidden' lock-log lines dropped a hide that followed a show. FIXED: one gate per kind.
Checked clean by the reviewer: threading (main looper only), penPending bookkeeping ("exactly one eval queued"), FingerGuard edges, LockLog cost (at most about 4 lines/s while locked).
