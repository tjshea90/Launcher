## BW1b — how the icon was built (nothing of the screenshots is stored)
`python3 -I tools/make_music_icon.py` writes the ADAPTIVE icon: `eleven/src/main/res/mipmap-{mdpi,hdpi,xhdpi,xxhdpi,xxxhdpi}/ic_launcher_foreground.png` (the 108 dp layer = 108 / 162 / 216 / 324 / 432 px, RGBA, only the speaker, everything else transparent), `mipmap-anydpi/ic_launcher.xml` and `values/ic_launcher_background.xml` (pure white). The generator holds only NUMBERS measured on the screenshots:
- **Geometry (drawer, 600x1024, plate 55 px):** speaker 39.3 px wide, centre 412.7 / 582.8 (the white plate is 55 x 55 at x 386-440, y 556-610); outer radius 19.67 px; thin black ring at radius 15.5-16.7; dust cap radius 4.0 with a darker edge ring to 5.3.
- **Colour model (least squares per 0.5 px radius over every angle, residual 2-5 levels):** cone = M(r) + D(r) * f with the SAME D in red, green and blue (a white glow from below: the cone is light at the bottom, dark at the top; f = dy / r); bezel = grey M(r) + D(r) * f lighter on top (top 131 / bottom 62 at r = 18); dust cap = 38.3 + 11.8 * (height above the centre in px), black at the bottom.
- **Fit:** the drawn speaker at the drawer's own size on a white plate differs from his screenshot by 4.8 levels of 255 on average (95th percentile 18) over 1400 pixels. **Independent check:** the same drawing at the size of the Files screenshot (33.5 px, nothing tuned to it) differs by 6.4. `python3 -I tools/make_music_icon.py --compare <drawer screenshot> 412.71 582.84 <out.png>` repeats the first check (the screenshot must be supplied; it is never stored here).
- **Adaptive, not legacy (what CI run #554 taught, 2026-11-11 18:34Z):** the first version was a LEGACY PNG (round artwork at 92 % of its canvas). Launcher3 wraps a legacy icon on its white plate and runs it through `IconNormalizer`, which shrinks a round icon so that it ends at most about 0.64 of the plate (0.70 `LEGACY_ICON_SCALE` x the normaliser's area scale, at most 0.916): the real drawer on the emulator showed our speaker at 0.62-0.65 of the plate (check MU-21 measured 34 of 52 px, 0.654; an eye measure said 0.617), his is 0.715 (39.34 of 55 px). So his own icon cannot be a round legacy PNG, and ours must not be either. An ADAPTIVE icon is not shrunk: the 108 dp foreground layer shows its middle 72 dp (= the plate; both plate sizes were checked on the pictures: pure-white box = the real bounds, the edge pixels are sharp), so a speaker of `R_OUT / 55 * 72 / 108` = 0.2384 of the layer as radius is exactly 71.5 % of the plate at every density. The background layer is pure white (`#FFFFFF`), like his plate. Upstream's pink-note layers stay deleted.
