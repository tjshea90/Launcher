# CV1 audit A: Mode dependence map (read-only subagent, HEAD be84f62e, 2026-11-22)

Tags: [V] = only reads the stored mode value, [SV] = also assumes the stock launcher (com.android.launcher3) is the thing on screen, resumed or owning the window, [B] = other.
Paths are relative to app/src/main/java/com/tj/launcher/ unless noted. About 150 `Mode.*` reads in 34 files, plus pure rule classes that take a `customMode` / `quickstepMode` boolean.

## [SV] DETAIL: what breaks if the visible QUICKSTEP-mode home were this app's own Launcher activity instead of the stock one

1. **Stray custom-home guard (TjHooks.java:87-92 -> TjAccessibilityService.java:3152-3188; StrayCustomGuard.java:27,54).** Premise "our Launcher3 resumed while mode = QUICKSTEP = stray". It would fire at every resume and send `startRouter("stock")`, up to 4 times per 8 s, then stand down for 30 s; a STRAYCUSTOM log line each time.
2. **Handshake and unlock into Quickstep (1798-1808, 1996-1998, 2062-2077, 3357-3380; ProfileSession.java:66,69).** `startQuickstepFront` starts the remembered app or the stock home through the router. "Quickstep in front" is `isForeground(stock)` or the resumed app. `verifyQuickstepFront` treats "our package in front" as a failure and restarts Quickstep: with our own home it would loop through both look attempts and log a false failure. The pre-arm of the custom home behind the lock screen (:1835-1842, router "custom") is the same activity, so the handshake would no longer REPLACE it.
3. **Router (RouterActivity.java:52,76-120).** Everything non-custom falls to `startStock`. The "stock" extra, `startStock` and the double-Home flip assume two different activities. The HOME-type task and its Recents exclusion apply only to the custom path.
4. **Stock-home guards (TjAccessibilityService.java:3101-3124; StrayHomeGuard.java:21-33).** `guardStrayHome` fights a stock HOME window in custom mode. With no stock home in QS mode it would be inert there, still active in custom.
5. **Resume target (3329-3339; ResumeTarget.java:16,22-25).** "He went home" is detected only by the stock package. Pressing Home onto our own activity would not clear `qsApp`, and `remember` also rejects our own package. A stale app (up to 6 h old) would be resumed at the next gesture unlock.
6. **Own-package filter (TjAccessibilityService.java:83).** Events of our package return before `trackDirty`, `guardStrayHome`, `noteOtherWindow` and the pencil scheduling. The own home in QS mode would be invisible to them.
7. **Own-icon hiding and entry points (AppVisibility.java:26-30; ControlActivity.java:48; AndroidManifest.xml:251-285).** Our Launcher3 drawer hides our own package's launcher icons (Control, Back to custom) and Control, Hidden apps, Setup and Adb screens finish in custom. Control, the Back-to-custom icon and Hidden apps are launched from the stock drawer. An own-launcher QS mode needs those entries visible (the filter is not mode-aware).
8. **Crash breaker and failsafes (CrashGuard.java:62-66; ProfileSession.java:103; RouterActivity.java:61).** The escape from a crash loop or a dead helper is "go to the stock launcher", a separate process and package that keeps working when ours crashes. QUICKSTEP mode in our own process would not escape a crash loop of our process.
9. **Quickstep-session bookkeeping (Mode.java:106-109; StockRefresh, StockForceStop, QsMemory).** They exist because the stock process grows during a Quickstep session. Those triggers would never fire for the stock process without stock home use. The stock process still hosts Recents in both modes, so the Recents scans (4028-4064, 4116, 4171, 4236; RecentsFilter.java:90) and `noteHiddenForSweep`'s `atHome = ... stockPkg()` (2411) stay stock-based.
10. **RecentsPolicy.apply (RecentsPolicy.java:34).** Launches from the Launcher3 activity are marked as the custom world's apps (`AppOrigin.markCustom`) in either mode. In QS mode that would mislabel Quickstep-session apps as custom, so they would be exempt from the return clean-up.
11. **`isForeground(getPackageName())` uses (settleHome 2372, verifyQuickstepFront 2069, StockForceStop `homeFront` 5292, the hotspot at 3392 via `ReturnReset.targetPackage`).** They read "our package in front = custom home". That would be true in both modes.

**Mode-agnostic as written ([V], fine with an own-activity QS home).** Tiles, keyboard, notifications, radios, animations, battery saver, immersive, VPN (all keyed on the stored value plus lock / screen / helper facts), the lock-screen and home patches, Settings look-alike, hidden-app filtering, crash breaker bookkeeping, reports.
