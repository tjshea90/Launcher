## (1) SAFETY mechanisms that need the stock launcher to be a SEPARATE, independent home app
1. Router fail-open (RouterActivity.java:38-41, :100-105). `StockLauncher.choose` skips our own package (StockLauncher.java:17-19). With no other HOME app there is nothing to fall back to (:110 only logs).
2. Crash breaker (CrashGuard.java:61-67): only flips Mode; the destination is the stock home started by the router (RouterActivity.java:105). Also relies on the stock app running in a different process from ours.
3. Failsafes: L10 triple Home, Konami, Solitaire hotspot, entrances, "Restore everything": all end in `startStock` or Mode QUICKSTEP.
4. "Choose a Home app" (RouterActivity.java:78-80, AdbGuideActivity.java:42): the chooser can offer the stock launcher only because it is a second installed HOME app.
5. OS-level rungs: `pm disable-user`, uninstall, Safe mode, Settings Default Home (FailsafeKeys.java:11, tools/adb-restore.sh:16, scenario :275).
6. Stray guards' stand-down and fail-open (StrayHomeGuard.java:15-16, :29): they assume two homes can fight each other.
7. The Quickstep-only control and restore screens live in the stock drawer.
8. UsageGap and the boot rule assume Android can itself show the stock HOME task at an unlock.

## (3) What Android needs from the stock launcher regardless of the default home
- **Overview/Recents binding.** SystemUI binds the stock TouchInteractionService (action QUICKSTEP_SERVICE, directBootAware). With another app as default home, OverviewComponentObserver picks the fallback RecentsActivity in the stock process (research/CD2_AOSP_Q_LAUNCHER3_FACTS_2026-11-14.md Q1-Q3; research/CG1F_QUICKSTEP_AOSP_ANGLE_2026-11-17.md:29-80, a VERIFIED AOSP read; research/CD2_QUICKSTEP_RAM_AUDIT_2026-11-14.md:10). The ROM's `config_recentsComponentName` points at the stock Quickstep (research/DESIGN_2026-11-02.md:24-25). Our code relies on this daily: GLOBAL_ACTION_RECENTS (service :3968) and the stock-window Recents scans. No Quickstep or overview class exists in our APK. Emulator proof: SR5 and SF6 (scenario :5530, :5604).
- **Navigation.** The tablet uses 3-button navigation (TASKS.md:31, research/HANDOFF_2026-11-03.md:5). No code touches navigation mode. Gesture navigation on this ROM was never examined (unknown). Per the AOSP excerpt, the integrated overview is used only when the stock launcher is the default home (CG1F:51-57).
- **Process must stay alive and not be killed.** ResetPolicy.java:10, ShellProtocol.java:27-30. Deliberate restarts (STOCKRESTART, Settings Force stop) expect SystemUI to re-bind.
- **The observer re-runs on package changes of our package** (CD2 facts Q2), because it registers receivers for the default home's package.
- **Memory side effect:** the stock process hosts Recents plus Quickstep sessions and was measured at about 400 MB on Tj's tablet versus 48 MB when it is the real home (scenario :5108-5113, research/CG1F_QUICKSTEP_AOSP_ANGLE_2026-11-17.md).
- **OS fallback target:** a separate enabled HOME app, for Safe mode, uninstall or disable of ours.
